Website Scraping & Security Scanner: Oweru Tech Solutions

The flagship of the Oweru platform: a scan engine that audits any website and reports what is wrong and how to fix it. An evolving, hands-on project rather than a finished commercial product.

  • Website discovery: finds real local businesses from public map data and lists which have no website
  • Crawling: walks through the found site's pages, collecting links and content
  • Business information collection: extracts contacts, services, ratings and reviews from public pages
  • Health scanning: runs automated checks across security, mobile friendliness, speed and trust areas for a score
  • Potential weaknesses & recommendations: each failed check explains what was found, why it matters and a suggested fix
